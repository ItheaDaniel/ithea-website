How I look after personal information. Last updated 05/11/2026.

1. Summary

ITHEA is a one-person business run by Daniel Hickling. This notice explains what personal information I hold, where it comes from, why I use it, how long I keep it, and what rights you have. It covers people who visit my website, people who contact me, and people I contact about my services.

The main points:

  • I only keep the details I need to run ITHEA and to get in touch with businesses that may find my services useful.
  • If you ask me to stop contacting you, I will, and I will keep only enough information to make sure I don't contact you again.
  • My website doesn't use cookies or analytics.
  • I use AI tools, including Claude, in my work. Section 5 explains how.
  • To use any of your rights, or to ask a question, email info@ithea.co.uk.

3. What I hold and where it comes from

3.1 People I contact about my services

I keep a list of businesses I think my services could help. For each one, I may hold:

  • a contact name and job title;
  • the organisation's name, address, website and email address;
  • a link to a LinkedIn page;
  • a short note about the business and a suggested way to contact them;
  • the dates I got in touch, any reply, any follow-up I plan, and short notes about conversations.

This information comes from publicly available business information found online, such as company websites, and from personal recommendations. Most email addresses are general business inboxes, but some may be for a named person.

3.2 People who contact me

If you email me, I keep your message, my reply and whatever you choose to tell me, such as your name, organisation and contact details.

3.3 People who visit my website

My website doesn't use cookies or analytics. The company that hosts it may keep technical records, such as visitors' IP addresses, to run and secure the service. I don't see these.

4. Why I use your information

My legal basis is "legitimate interests". In plain terms, I have a genuine business reason to tell organisations about services that could help them, and to keep records of who I have been in touch with. I try to keep this to what a business would reasonably expect, and I make it easy to say no.

If I email you first, the message tells you how I found your details and how to ask me to stop. If you reply to say you don't want to hear from me, I won't contact you again.

5. AI tools and other services

I use AI tools, such as Claude, to help with research and to tidy up my notes. This can include business contact details found online and short notes about conversations with the business. I rely on each provider's own privacy terms for how they handle information.

My email and files are stored in the UK (Microsoft 365). Some of the other services I use are based outside the UK, for example in the USA. I rely on each provider's own privacy terms.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • ask to see the information I hold about you;
  • ask me to correct anything that is wrong;
  • ask me to delete your information;
  • object to me using your information, including for marketing.

To use any of these, email info@ithea.co.uk. I will reply within one month. If you ask me to stop contacting you, I will act on it straight away and keep only the short record described in section 6.

If you are not happy with how I have handled your information, please tell me first. You can also complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

8. Changes to this notice

I will review this notice at least once a year, and whenever one of my providers or the way I work changes. Daniel Hickling owns it. Questions go to info@ithea.co.uk.
